# Awesome-Third-Party-Risk-Management

## Top Third-Party Risk Management Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Vendor Risk Assessment, Security Questionnaires, Continuous Monitoring, Security Ratings & TPRM Lifecycle*

**Last updated: August 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM)**. These systems help organizations inventory vendors, issue and score security questionnaires, monitor external cyber risk, manage contracts and remediation, and maintain an ongoing vendor-risk program.



**Examples** include ProcessUnity, OneTrust, SecurityScorecard, BitSight, Whistic, Panorays, UpGuard, Black Kite, Prevalent, and Venminder (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Fully featured commercial TPRM suites are uncommon in pure open source, but practical platforms and toolkits now exist. **Fair TPRM**, **LT-VRM**, and related projects provide self-hostable alternatives for assessments, scoring, and vendor lifecycle management. This section is expanded with the strongest available open options.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[ProcessUnity](https://www.processunity.com/)**  

  Highly configurable enterprise TPRM platform focused on vendor lifecycle workflows, assessments, and risk management for large organizations.



- **[OneTrust](https://www.onetrust.com/)**  

  Broad GRC and privacy platform with strong third-party risk modules covering questionnaires, continuous monitoring, and compliance workflows.



- **[SecurityScorecard](https://securityscorecard.com/)**  

  Security ratings and continuous monitoring platform that provides portfolio-level cyber risk visibility into third parties.



- **[BitSight](https://www.bitsight.com/)**  

  Leading security ratings provider used for continuous third-party cyber risk monitoring and TPRM programs, especially in financial services.



- **[Whistic](https://www.whistic.com/)**  

  Vendor assessment and trust-exchange platform that streamlines security questionnaires and evidence sharing.



- **[Panorays](https://panorays.com/)**  

  Hybrid platform combining security assessments with attack-surface and external monitoring for third-party risk.



- **[UpGuard](https://www.upguard.com/)**  

  Third-party risk and external attack-surface monitoring platform with ratings, questionnaires, and vendor risk workflows.



- **[Black Kite](https://blackkite.com/)**  

  Cyber risk rating and intelligence platform focused on continuous monitoring of third-party security posture.



- **[Prevalent](https://www.prevalent.net/)**  

  Third-party risk management platform covering vendor assessments, continuous monitoring, and risk intelligence.



- **[Venminder](https://www.venminder.com/)**  

  Vendor risk management solution popular with financial institutions for due diligence, ongoing monitoring, and oversight.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Fair TPRM](https://www.fairtprm.com/)**  

  Free, open-source third-party risk management platform combining vendor lifecycle management, GRC compliance tracking, assessment templates (NIST, ISO, SOC 2, etc.), and FAIR-style risk quantification. Self-hostable.



- **[LT-VRM](https://github.com/learntprm-design/lt-vrm)**  

  Free & open-source TPRM / Vendor Risk Management platform supporting vendor assessments, security questionnaires, risk scoring (0–1000), contract management, breach monitoring, and a vendor portal. Designed for easy self-hosting.



- **[CISO Assistant](https://github.com/intuitem/ciso-assistant-community)**  

  Open-source GRC platform with compliance framework mapping, risk assessment capabilities, and growing support for vendor-related workflows.



- **[RiskHub](https://github.com/W1z4rd1c4/RiskHub)**  

  Open-source risk operations platform for governed risks, controls, KRIs, vendors, approvals, and evidence management.



- **[Openlane](https://github.com/theopenlane/core)**  

  Open-source compliance automation platform that includes vendor/entity registries, questionnaires, and roadmap items for vendor risk scoring and TPRM modules.



- **[Bandit – Vendor Privacy Risk Assessment](https://github.com/conorrusso/bandit)**  

  Open-source AI agent / CLI that scores vendor privacy policies against GDPR/CCPA-oriented dimensions and produces structured reports.



- **[TPRM program templates and risk registers](https://github.com/)**  

  Community repositories containing risk-based tiering models, due-diligence questionnaires, and vendor risk registers aligned to ISO 27001, DORA, and similar frameworks.



- **[Security questionnaire and evidence libraries](https://github.com/)**  

  Open collections of standardized questions (SIG, CAIQ-style, custom) and response templates usable in self-hosted assessment workflows.



- **[External scanning and rating open tools](https://github.com/)**  

  Open-source attack-surface and vulnerability scanners that can feed continuous monitoring data into a custom TPRM process.



- **[Workflow and approval engines for vendor onboarding](https://github.com/)**  

  Open workflow platforms used to orchestrate vendor onboarding, assessment, remediation, and offboarding steps.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Integration of open GRC tools with existing CMDB, contract, and procurement systems.

- Spreadsheet-to-database migration patterns for organizations moving off pure Excel TPRM.

- Open reporting templates for board and regulator-facing third-party risk summaries.

- Community-shared residual-risk and inherent-risk scoring methodologies.

- Lightweight vendor portals built on open form and document-sharing stacks.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Deploy **Fair TPRM** or **LT-VRM** as the core vendor inventory, assessment, and scoring engine. Supplement with open questionnaire libraries, external scanning results, and contract metadata from existing systems. Surface risk dashboards in open BI tools and manage remediation via tickets or workflow engines. This approach provides full data ownership and zero licensing cost for the TPRM core — suitable for mid-market teams and organizations with strong internal GRC capacity — while commercial platforms still dominate for large-scale continuous ratings networks, managed assessment exchanges, and extensive regulatory content libraries.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Third-party risk management involves legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations. Open-source tools provide transparency and control but still require proper process design, legal review of questionnaires and contracts, and alignment with applicable frameworks (ISO 27001, DORA, SOC 2, NIST, etc.). Always validate that your program meets the expectations of regulators, auditors, and customers.

- Security ratings and external scans are inputs, not complete substitutes for due diligence.
